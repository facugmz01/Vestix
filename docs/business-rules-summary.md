# Business Rules Summary

This document outlines the strict, non-negotiable business rules that the software must enforce, regardless of user input or offline state.

## 1. Catalog & Transactability
- **Variants Only**: Base `Products` (e.g., "T-Shirt") cannot be sold, bought, or counted. All physical operations occur strictly at the `ProductVariant` level (e.g., "T-Shirt, Red, XL").
- **SKU Uniqueness**: A Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) must be globally unique across the entire tenant.

## 2. Pricing & Trust
- **Zero Trust Client**: The POS/Client cannot dictate the final price of an item. It may only send the `variantId` and `quantity`. The backend `PricingService` and `RulesEngine` are the absolute source of truth for base prices and promotional discounts.
- **Manual Overrides**: If a cashier applies a manual discount, it must explicitly match an authorized `discountPct` allowed by their RBAC role.

## 3. Inventory & Stock
- **No Negative Ledger**: `StockMovement.quantity` must always be a strictly positive integer. A negative adjustment is achieved by setting the `type` to `SHRINKAGE` and reversing the source/destination warehouses.
- **Warehouse Isolation**: Physical stock exists only inside a `Warehouse`. A `Branch` has no physical stock; its stock is the mathematical sum of its assigned Warehouses (e.g., Storefront + Backroom).
- **Reservations**: E-commerce orders do not deplete physical stock immediately. They increment `reservedQuantity` and decrement `availableQuantity`. Physical stock drops only upon shipping.

## 4. Sales & Idempotency
- **Client-Generated IDs**: Every `SaleOrder` must possess a `UUIDv4` generated by the frontend at the moment of creation. If the backend receives the same ID twice, it must silently return success (Idempotency) without double-charging or double-decrementing stock.

## 5. Finance & Current Accounts
- **Credit Limits**: B2B Customers have a hard `creditLimit`. A sale utilizing `CUSTOMER_CREDIT` must be rejected if `usedCredit + orderTotal > creditLimit`.
- **Treasury Routing**: Every cash or credit card sale must be explicitly routed to a specific `TreasuryAccount` (e.g., "Cash Register 1", "Main Bank Account"). Money does not exist in a void.

## 6. Fiscal & AFIP Integration
- **Decoupled Invoicing**: Generating an Electronic Invoice (Factura) is a separate lifecycle from a Sale Order. A sale is valid operationally the moment the goods are exchanged. An invoice can be generated synchronously, asynchronously, or batched at the end of the day.
